The custom gate excuses a reading only where it can establish that the player hides what the reading measures. For a custom part that declares `occlusion`, at a pose where the part is fully hidden across the stretches to the neighbouring sampled values of the key parameter on both sides, `TIE` and `SLIDE` leave the pose out when the whole ring around the tie lies under the paint of an occluder the player draws at full alpha, and `EXPOSED` does not count a hidden-art pixel under that paint. An occluder that fades (an `opacity` binding, a transform opacity or a colour alpha under 1) gives no cover; an amount under 1 is judged in full; `FOLDS`, `RIGID` and `TIP` read every pose. A new `CUSTOM <id> HIDDEN:` line, after `EXPOSED`, reports the occluders and which give cover, the amount at rest, where it leaves its rest value and reaches 1, and how many poses `TIE` and `SLIDE` left out; it never fails the gate, and a part without `occlusion` reads as before. The `gate` result carries it as `hidden` on the part.
