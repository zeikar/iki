`list_standard_parameters` lists `ParamAmbientSway` (29 entries): the slow zero-mean drift the engine's idle writes for physics rigs to follow. Rigged models declare it, and a spring's `PHYSICS` line lists every input of its rig. The bundled engine (the `render_poses` page and the physics report) plays a rig's `lag` inputs and steps each input of a multi-input spring alone, reporting the run that moves the parts most.
