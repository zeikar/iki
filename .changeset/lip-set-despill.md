The lip set carries no green fringe. The key leaves green in an antialiased edge pixel it has no ramp to read (a halo off the line from the ink to the fill, or a share it takes for the stroke's own shading), and the resize spreads it, so `lip_upper` and `mouth_inner` showed a green rim at every opening. `compose_layers_from_parts` and `cut_layers_from_illustration` now despill the colours the split writes within 3 px of the opening (its boundary and the neighbourhood the resize spreads the key over): a pixel's green is clamped to the larger of its red and blue, alpha untouched. The split still reads the frame as keyed, so no read of its shape or the fold's contract changes, and the interior's own colours and paint the key never touched (teal lip shading away from the opening) pass through as drawn. The measure's green check reads what is drawn on the opening's edge: a line pixel within 3 px of the cavity whose excess green at its own alpha is over 16 levels, where it read only opaque pixels over 40, which passed the fringe; flat key green (opaque, over 40) is still flagged anywhere, and the interior's own fill is never read. Its remedy is recomposing (free) before regenerating, and it names `mouth_interior.png` beside `mouth_keyed.png` when `mouth_inner` is the layer flagged.
