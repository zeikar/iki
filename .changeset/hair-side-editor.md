Side locks: the auto-rig takes optional `hair_side_L` / `hair_side_R` roles (family `hair_side`), front locks drawn over the face, the brows and the lashes and under `hair_front`. Each needs `hair_front`; a set with a lock and no front hair is refused (`auto-rig: hair_side_L needs a hair_front layer`). A lock is a root-hinged shear: down to the row where it first shows under the bangs it moves exactly as the front hair, then it eases over 0.2 head heights to the face plate's own motion and keeps its width below. It shares the front hair's two sway springs — the bangs' sway at the root, a root-pinned sway of its own below, each tip travelling the bangs' amplitude — and the roll's hang, which develops over at least one head height below the chin, so a lock ending near the chin rolls almost rigidly with the head. `rowRuns` are measured for both roles. `TurnSolveReport.hairSide` (the new exported `HairSideRoom`, `left` / `right` by screen side) reports, for each lock at its far stops, how far its outer edge stays outside the head's edge (the face plate's, but beneath an ear; `cover`) and where the far eye's and lash's outer corner sit against its face-side edge and that edge (`tail`); the numbers are report-only, a metric that cannot be read is omitted, and nothing bounds the turn by them. A layer set without the roles rigs byte-identically.
