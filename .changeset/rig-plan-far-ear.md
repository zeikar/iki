New rig-plan field `turn.farEar`: how far the far ear's outer edge follows the face on a head turn, as a share of the face plate's slide (0 to 1, default 0). At 0 the ear holds and the head slides over it, so about half of a default-sized ear goes under the face; at 1 it rides with the face and stays whole. The default is unchanged, so a rig with no rig plan comes out the same.
