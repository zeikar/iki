Depends on the new `@ikijs/editor`, so a model it rigs from this release moves as that release's changeset describes: where its hair layers carry `rowRuns`, the front hair's cap on the turn goes no further outside the back hair than the nod alone puts it, and where its plate paints ear islands, the far ear narrows to 0.83 of its width at ±30 where the head leaves it room, and less where it does not (a fitted turn that would narrow the head too far to cover it is limited, and reported as clamped). The `.iki` format does not change, and models rigged earlier are untouched until they are rigged again.
